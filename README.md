# Food Catcher

Food Catcher is a donation platform designed to help support people experiencing homelessness in New York City by making charitable giving more visible, transparent, and engaging.

The goal of Food Catcher is to encourage more people to donate by showing the direct impact their contributions can have. Instead of donating without knowing where the money goes, users can see updates and photos showing how donations were used to provide meals and other support to people in need.

## Our Mission

---

Many people want to help their community but may hesitate to donate because they are unsure how their money will be used.

Food Catcher aims to create greater trust between donors and donation efforts by providing visibility into the impact of each contribution.

Through the platform, users can:

* Donate toward meals and resources for people experiencing homelessness in NYC
* Learn about active donation efforts
* See how donations are being used
* View photos and updates showing the real-world impact of donations
* Encourage others to contribute and support their local community
